Why I Started Writing and Creating Games and Apps

On curiosity, early influences, and why this portfolio exists.

When I first became interested in technology as a child, I was fascinated by how games, websites, and apps were made. I spent countless hours playing games such as Tarzan, Virtua Cop 2, Age of Empires, Time Machine, and Cricket 97. Over time, instead of just playing them, I became curious about how they worked behind the scenes.

Learning HTML and CSS was one of my first steps into development. It showed me that anyone can create something for the web with enough curiosity, patience, and practice. As I learned more, I began coming up with my own app ideas and game concepts.

WHAT I LOVE ABOUT DESIGN

One thing I enjoy about game design is that it combines creativity with problem-solving. Every game starts with an idea, but turning that idea into something playable requires planning, testing, and continuous improvement.

This portfolio and blog is a place where I share my projects, thoughts, game ideas, and experiences as I continue learning and building. Some projects may succeed, while others may fail, but every project teaches something valuable.

My goal is simple: to keep creating, keep learning, and share my journey with others who are interested in technology, games, and development.

Thank you for reading, and I hope you enjoy following my progress.
