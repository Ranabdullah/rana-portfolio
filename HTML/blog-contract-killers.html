Project Concept: Contract Killers

A top-down real-time tactics game about running a secret contract-killing organization.

Unreal Engine Tactics / Strategy Low-Poly Branching Narrative
Overview

Contract Killers is a top-down real-time tactics and strategy game inspired by Commandos, cyberpunk-themed tactical games, and stealth-based assassination mechanics. The player runs a secret contract-killing organization that accepts missions for money, each with different objectives, budgets, and challenges. Success depends on choosing the right specialists for the job.

The game is planned for PC using Unreal Engine and features low-poly graphics, tactical gameplay, character progression, and multiple story paths.

Story

Anton Hills is a highly trained assassin working for a secret underground organization led by Oliver Butler, who runs a hidden company that recruits killers blending into society as ordinary citizens — doctors, engineers, chefs, police officers, hackers, lawyers, and more.

Anton's wife, Scarlett Hills, accidentally leaks information about the organization to the police. At this point, the player faces a defining choice:

OPTION 1 — ELIMINATE SCARLETT

Anton loses his family and begins a personal revenge mission against those responsible for destroying his life.

OPTION 2 — PROTECT SCARLETT

The organization marks Scarlett for death. Multiple assassins are sent after her, and Anton must protect his wife while fighting former allies.

If Anton dies, his son John Hills continues the story. Although not a member of the organization, he was trained by his father and seeks revenge.

Main Antagonists

Oliver Butler — Leader of the underground assassination organization.

Patricia Hampton — Secretary and high-ranking member who manages sensitive operations.

Gameplay & Specialists

The player manages a roster of specialists. Each has unique abilities and costs money to hire, but only five operatives can be taken on a mission at a time — better specialists demand higher payments. Characters gain experience and improve skills through successful missions.

  • Sniper: Long-range eliminations and reconnaissance.
  • Hacker: Disabling security cameras, unlocking doors, creating diversions.
  • Infiltrator: Silent takedowns and lockpicking.
  • Heavy Weapons: Direct assault and armored distraction.
  • Medic / Chemist: Poison synthesis, tranquilizers, and team recovery.
