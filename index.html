About Rana

Rana Abdullah is a multidisciplinary creative technologist based in Dublin, Ireland — a novelist, 3D artist, software developer, and brand strategist whose work bridges deep narrative storytelling and high-performance technical execution.

With over a decade of hands-on experience spanning 3D visualization, literary fiction, workflow automation tools, and commercial media production for international clients, every project begins with a defining question: what does this need to feel like, and how does it function at its absolute best?

Author of eight novels and novellas. Creator of widely deployed Blender workflow add-ons and full-stack web applications.
